What if I’m completely new to yoga?

Absolutely welcome! My classes are designed for all levels. You’ll learn foundational poses, proper breathing, and safe alignment so you can build confidence step by step.

Do I need special equipment?

All you need to start is a yoga mat and comfortable clothing. Blocks, straps, or bolsters are optional but helpful—I'll show easy alternatives if you don’t have them.

How much time do I need each week?

Just 10–20 minutes a day can make a noticeable difference. If you prefer longer sessions, I also offer 30–60 minute classes so you can choose what fits your schedule.

Will this help with stress, flexibility, or back pain?

Yes! Yoga supports flexibility, improves posture, reduces stress, and can help relieve tension in the back and neck. Many students notice improvements within the first few weeks.

What happens after I sign up?

You’ll get instant access to your welcome kit, beginner videos, and weekly guided sessions. You’ll also receive support emails, reminders, and tips to help you stay consistent and motivated.
