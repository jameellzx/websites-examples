Is a water flosser better than traditional flossing?

A water flosser is an excellent alternative to traditional flossing, especially for people with braces, dental implants, or gum disease. While traditional floss is effective at physically scraping plaque off teeth, water flossers are more comfortable for some users and can reach deep gum pockets more easily.

Can a water flosser help with gum disease?

Yes, a water flosser can be very beneficial for individuals with gum disease (gingivitis or periodontitis). It helps remove bacteria and food particles from below the gumline, reducing inflammation and promoting healthier gums.

How often should I use a water flosser?

It is recommended to use a water flosser at least once a day, preferably before or after brushing your teeth, to maintain optimal oral hygiene and prevent plaque buildup.
